Hunt

    Giant Squid. Little is known of the giant squid's hunting habits. They are so rarely seen because scientists who go to the deep ocean have bright lights on the submersible. The giant squid has eyes so large they are sensitive to light. In 2025, fishermen in the ocean near Japan finally caught the giant squid eating a fish as they brought the fish aboard. The squid releases the fish and returns to the deep.
